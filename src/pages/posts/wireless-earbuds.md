## Features Explained

### Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)

> "ANC is life-changing for open offices."

How it works:
- Microphones capture ambient noise
- Inverse sound waves cancel noise
- Result: Near silence 🫢

### Transparency Mode

Hear your surroundings without removing earbuds:
- Great for quick conversations
- Stay aware of your environment
- Perfect for working from coffee shops
