# Agentic AI: The Rise of Autonomous AI Agents in 2026

The AI landscape has shifted dramatically. **Agentic AI** - autonomous AI agents that can plan, execute, and iterate on tasks - is no longer science fiction. It's 2026, and these digital workers are transforming how we code, create, and conduct business.

---

## What is Agentic AI?

> "Agentic AI = AI that doesn't just answer questions - it takes action."

Unlike traditional AI (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.), Agentic AI can:

- 🔍 **Research** autonomously
- 📝 **Plan** multi-step workflows
- 🛠️ **Execute** tasks across apps
- 🔄 **Iterate** based on results
- 🤝 **Collaborate** with other agents
