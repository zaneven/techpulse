Your monitor is your **window into your code**. For 8+ hours of daily use, having the right display isn't a luxury - it's a necessity. Here are the best monitors for programmers.

## What Matters for Coding

### The Big Four

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| **Resolution** | More screen real estate = less tab switching |
| **Ergonomics** | Height, tilt, pivot adjustments |
| **Eye comfort** | Blue light filter, no flicker |
| **Connectivity** | USB-C, HDMI, DisplayPort |

### Recommended Specs

| Spec | Minimum | Ideal |
|------|---------|-------|
| Size | 24" | 27" |
| Resolution | 1080p | 1440p or 4K |
| Panel | IPS | IPS |
| Refresh | 60Hz | 75Hz+ |

## Setting Up Your Monitor

### Optimal Positioning

```
     👁️ Eye Level
        ┌─────────┐
        │  Monitor │
        └─────────┘
          \   |   /
           \  |  /
      ~20-30 inches away
```

- **Height:** Top of screen at eye level
- **Distance:** Arm's length (~20-30")
- **Tilt:** 10-20° back

## Connection Types

| Port | Best For | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **USB-C** | Modern laptops | Data + power + video |
| **HDMI** | Consoles, older PCs | Common |
| **DisplayPort** | High refresh | Best for 144Hz+ |

> **Pro tip:** USB-C reduces cable clutter significantly.
