## Setup Guide

### Step 1: Position Your Desk

```
     ┌─────────────┐
     │   Monitor   │
     │  [centered] │
     ├─────────────┤
     │  Keyboard   │
     ├─────────────┤
     │    Mouse    │
     └─────────────┘
           ↑
      20-30" from eyes
```

- **Monitor:** Arm's length away
- **Top of screen:** At eye level
- **Keyboard:** Elbows at 90°

---

### Step 2: Cable Management
